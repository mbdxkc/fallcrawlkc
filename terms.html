    Terms

    This site tells you about a neighborhood event. It is free to attend, there is nothing to buy here and there is nothing to sign.

    The event

    The Fall Crawl is a self-guided crawl through the East Crossroads. There are no tickets, no wristbands and no set route. Nothing on this site is a ticket or a reservation, and no purchase is required to take part.

    Every venue runs its own night

    Participating businesses operate independently. Each one sets and runs its own hours, specials, events, entertainment, service and premises, and each one is responsible for them. Specials and line-ups can change or end without notice. If something matters to your plans, check with that venue directly.

    Listing a business here is not a partnership, a guarantee or an endorsement of it, and linking to another site is not an endorsement of that site.

    Alcohol and age

    You must be 21 or older to purchase or consume alcohol. Bring ID. Venues serve at their own discretion and can refuse service. Please drink responsibly and arrange a safe way home.

    Taking part is up to you

    You attend at your own risk, in public streets and private businesses that we do not control. Follow each venue’s rules and its staff.

    Accuracy

    We keep the details here as current as we can, and they come from the businesses themselves. Dates, venues and offers can change. The information is provided as it is, without a guarantee that it is complete or current.

    Artwork and words

    The Fall Crawl name, logo, artwork and the copy on this site belong to their owners. Please do not reuse them without asking. Business names and logos belong to those businesses.
